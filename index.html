L low / high graphics
[ / ] mouse sensitivity

Click shoot your line & grab

Pallets grab one and it flies where you look. Stand on it first to ride it! Z/X = slower / faster

How things interact

TNT, barrels & mines set each other off in chains
Fire spreads across wood and cooks off explosives
Glass shatters, wood splinters, stone crumbles
Frozen things break way easier and thaw in explosions
Things float or sink in water depending on material
Dummies ragdoll when hit, then get back up
Balloons lift whatever they're tied to
Jump pads launch anything that touches them
